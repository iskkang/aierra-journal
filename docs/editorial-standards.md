# Aierra’s Journal — internal editorial standards

Internal authoring instructions. Do not render this document as a public website section.

## Voice
Write in natural, precise, measured British-English editorial prose. Prefer clear statements, strong transitions and concrete evidence over AI-sounding boilerplate. Avoid inflated adjectives, repetitive summaries, mechanical listicles and generic optimism.

## Separation of content
- News: verified factual reporting, dated, with attributable primary sources.
- Policy: explain what the rule says, whom it affects, jurisdiction, effective date and exceptions. Link to the official text.
- Editorial: original interpretation connecting transactions, context and policy; distinguish observed facts from inference.
- Market data: describe sample, source, coverage, filters, methodology, last refresh and revision risks.

## Integrity
Never invent journalists, credentials, interviews, quotations, source material, statistics, price forecasts or first-hand reporting. Do not attach a real person's byline without authorization. Attribute institutional editorial work to Aierra’s Journal Editorial. Explain AI-assisted production transparently in the site's publishing policy, not by inserting 'AI' into every title. Use images only with appropriate rights and avoid claiming a stock image depicts a specific location unless verified.

## Editorial checklist
Every numerical claim must have a traceable source and observation date. Separate median transaction prices from repeat-sales indices, asking prices, rents and affordability. Explain registration lag and compositional effects. Do not publish fabricated chart points or misleading comparison periods. Use a clear thesis, an evidential middle and a conclusion proportional to the evidence.
